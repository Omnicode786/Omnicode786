## About Me

I am an **AI and full-stack developer** from Karachi who turns ambitious ideas into usable products. My work sits at the intersection of **agentic AI, RAG, voice systems, modern web platforms, business operations, and interactive experiences**.

I enjoy owning the complete build: understanding the problem, planning the architecture, designing the workflow, creating the interface, connecting the database, adding intelligence, and documenting the system properly.

- Building **AI-first products**, not isolated chatbot demos
- Strong focus on **Next.js, TypeScript, Python, PostgreSQL, Prisma, APIs, and production workflows**
- Hands-on with **LLMs, embeddings, semantic search, vector databases, tool calling, guardrails, and voice AI**
- Interested in **product engineering, startups, game development, civic technology, robotics, and intelligent automation**

> **Current mission:** build software that feels polished on the surface and remains genuinely useful underneath.

### 🎙️ All-Purpose AI Voice Operations Agent

A reusable **RAG-backed voice-agent system** built to adapt to different organizations and call workflows instead of serving only one narrow use case.

It can be configured for:

- **Customer service calls** and FAQ resolution
- **Lead qualification**, follow-ups, and appointment booking
- **HR screening calls**, structured interview questions, and onboarding support
- **Internal help desks** and company knowledge assistance
- **Service calls**, status updates, reminders, and multilingual voice workflows

The underlying system combines **speech input/output, ElevenLabs voice, OpenAI or local Ollama models, embeddings, vector retrieval, tool use, guardrails, conversation memory, and human handoff logic**.
